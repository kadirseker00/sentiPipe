Understanding multilingual sentiment, one component at a time.

A study of language identification, translation, sentiment classification and topic modelling in multilingual user reviews, evaluated using M-ABSA and MARC.

Questions this study investigates

The paper compares individual components within a shared evaluation framework. These questions connect its experiments to the decisions involved in building a multilingual analysis system.

How does translation affect multilingual sentiment analysis?

The study evaluates translation systems alongside sentiment classification, including analysis of original reviews and English translations. Interpret the comparisons within the experiment's sampling and reference-translation design.

Paper: Sections 4.2–4.3 ↗

How do LLMs compare with traditional language identification models?

Language identification is compared on M-ABSA and MARC. Results differ by dataset, and combining model predictions does not always improve on an individual model.

Paper: Section 4.1 ↗

Where do errors and coverage limitations appear in a multilingual NLP pipeline?

The evaluation examines language confusion, noisy reviews, translation differences and sentiment errors. Component-level results reveal different strengths, failure patterns and coverage across the evaluated settings.
